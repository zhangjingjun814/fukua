About the Song

“Exaggerated” was written by Wyman Wong, with music and arrangement by C.Y. Kong, and appears on Eason Chan's 2005 album U87. In the first person, it tells the story of someone who feels ordinary, even invisible, and tries to win attention through exaggeration. The emotion builds step by step, and the near-screaming finale has made it, for many listeners, a defining work of Cantonese pop.
