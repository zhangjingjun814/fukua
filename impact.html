Notable Covers

The song is technically demanding and emotionally intense, which makes it a challenge many singers welcome. Hua Chenyu, Zhou Shen and JJ Lin have each covered it on different stages and shows, bringing their own interpretations.

Hua Chenyu

A dramatic, personal rework that amplifies the song's neurotic tension.

Zhou Shen

Known for delicate tone and control, revealing more restrained emotional layers.

JJ Lin

Adds his own melodic language in live performances, highlighting technical finish.

Cultural Legacy

“Exaggerated” is one of Eason Chan's defining works and is often treated as an entry point into Cantonese pop. At his concerts it has repeatedly become a moment of whole-crowd connection, and its themes of ordinariness and the need to exist keep it being played, discussed and re-interpreted more than twenty years after its release.

For learners, the song is also a fine example of how melody, arrangement, lyrics and performance work together around a single theme to create a complete work.
