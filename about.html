The Story: A Timeline

  • 2003

    After Leslie Cheung's passing, his collaborator C.Y. Kong wrote a melody first titled Depression. It expressed his grief and his hope that people would notice those struggling around them.

  • 2003 – 2005

    The melody was set aside. Eason Chan hoped to write his own lyrics, but after several drafts he remained unsatisfied, and the task eventually went to Wyman Wong.

  • 2005

    Instead of continuing the memorial, Wyman Wong turned it into the cry of an overlooked everyman and titled the song “Exaggerated.”

  • June 7, 2005

    The song was released with the album U87, becoming one of its defining tracks.

Three Key People

Eason Chan

The performer. From restrained narration to an emotional explosion, his vocal intensity brings the anxious everyman in the song to life.

Wyman Wong

The lyricist. He transformed a memorial melody into a story about ordinariness and the need to exist, giving the song universal resonance.

C.Y. Kong

Composer and arranger. First written in 2003, the building arrangement paves the way for the climactic finale.
